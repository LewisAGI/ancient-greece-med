# ancient-greece-med content pack (v3, 2026-10-22)

The site reads `content.json` and `sources.json`. Field rules are in [`SCHEMA.md`](SCHEMA.md).

The source file has 125 entries and 53 sources: 6 periods, 41 myths, 40 sites, 12 people, 20 timeline rows, and 6 further-reading pointers. Six entries are flagged `include: false` (Alexandria, Emporion, Massalia, and their timeline rows) because they rest on search summaries. The published catalog has 119 entries. Seventeen sites have coordinates, including Abu Mena and Lugo.

Coordinates exist only where a UNESCO listing gives them, converted from DMS. `null` means not sourced. UNESCO points are site centroids. Only published entries with coordinates are pinned.

Primary texts are public domain:

- Homer, Iliad and Odyssey, translated by Samuel Butler, Project Gutenberg.
- Hesiod and the Homeric Hymns, translated by H.G. Evelyn-White (1914), Project Gutenberg.
- Pausanias, Description of Greece, translated by W.H.S. Jones (1918), Books 1.17–29, 2.1–14, 5.1–15, and 10.1–16.

Badges are Evidence, Inference, and Abstract. A label that names both evidence and inference shows both.

## Gaps

Period dates are conventions (the Met and Pyrgos differ on the Archaic start). Several UNESCO serial sites have no single coordinate. Odyssey line numbers are still book-level. Pausanias 1.1–16, 1.30–44, 5.16–27, and 10.17–38 were not read. Alexandria, Emporion, and Massalia stay out of the build until a page has been read.
