# ancient-greece-med content pack (v2, 2026-10-22)

The site reads `content.json` and `sources.json`. Field rules are in [`SCHEMA.md`](SCHEMA.md).

Counts: periods 6, myths_places 22, sites 28 (21 of them new, 14 outside Greece). 36 sources. 56 entries in all.

Coordinates exist only where a UNESCO listing gives them, converted from DMS. `null` means not sourced. UNESCO points are site centroids. Only entries with coordinates are pinned. The others, including sites outside Greece that lack a point, are listed and not pinned.

Primary texts are public domain:

- Homer, Iliad and Odyssey, translated by Samuel Butler, Project Gutenberg.
- Hesiod and the Homeric Hymns, translated by H.G. Evelyn-White (1914), Project Gutenberg.
- Pausanias, Description of Greece 2.1–14, translated by W.H.S. Jones (1918).

Labels on the badge are `evidence`, `inference`, or `abstract-only`. This pack’s entries are `evidence`.

## Gaps

Period dates are conventions (the Met and Pyrgos differ on the Archaic start). Eleven sites have no coordinate because UNESCO did not show DMS, including serial sites such as Mycenae and the Minoan palaces. Pausanias Books 1, 5, and 10 (Athens, Olympia, Delphi) are not in the pack. Odyssey place identifications are mythical and flagged uncertain. Still missing further west and south: Egypt (Alexandria), Iberia, and Marseille (Massalia).

Troy, Syracuse, and Carthage are in this pack, so the earlier example placeholders for them are gone.
